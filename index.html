Creating Fara Low budget · Big impact

I believe a project needs a clear, coherent identity with purpose. Not just something that sets it apart, but a narrative that pushes it beyond its own limits.

That vision gave birth to Fara, a symbolic figure representing ambition, strength and resilience. Not a mascot — an identity: a concept that runs through players, team and community, expressing the competitive essence of the project.

Through this narrative I built a universe of its own, with mythology and meaning, connecting with the audience on an emotional and distinctive level.

Built on a lean budget — proof that a strong idea, the right talent and tight execution can deliver results far beyond the investment.
